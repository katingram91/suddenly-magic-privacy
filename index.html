Privacy Policy for Suddenly, Magic!

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Suddenly, Magic! ("the game") is made by Kat Craft Studios.

The short version: the game collects no personal information.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress (spells, students, resources, settings) is saved on your device only. It is not sent anywhere by the game.
  • The game does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • The game does not use the internet. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not share anything with third parties.

The play report

The game keeps a short log of your recent visits (what you earned, what you bought, which spells have students) on your device only, so that the "Copy play report" and "Send feedback" buttons in Settings can describe what happened. It is not sent anywhere unless you tap one of those buttons and choose to share the text yourself.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game asks Android to show you a reminder (for example, that a quest has finished). These reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Backups

Android may back up an app's saved data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. That is a feature of your device and your Google account, controlled in Android's settings, and Kat Craft Studios does not receive or see that data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes its saved data from your device. The game also has a "Start New Game" option in Settings that erases your progress.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
